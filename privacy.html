HushDraw

Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

HushDraw collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDK of any kind. The app has no server to send anything to. This page exists because the App Store requires every app to have one, not because there is much to say.

What is collected

Nothing. HushDraw does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage data, device identifier, or contact detail. There is no sign-up, no way to create an account, and no network connection of any kind.

What is stored, and where

The app keeps your draws, the people in them, your saved groups, any word sets you write or edit, and your settings on your device, using Apple's standard app storage. None of it is sent to us. We have no server, and there is nothing for us to look at. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Contacts

If you tap Add from Contacts, HushDraw shows Apple's own contact picker. That picker runs outside the app, so the app never gets access to your address book — which is why iOS does not ask you for contacts permission. The app receives only the single person you choose, and only the name, email, or phone number attached to them. That detail is saved on your device alongside the draw, and is used for one thing: filling in the message you choose to send.

Messages and email

When you send someone their name or their part of a round, HushDraw opens Apple's standard message or mail composer with the text already written. You press send, from your own phone, through your own carrier or mail account. The app cannot send anything by itself, and nothing passes through us.

What leaves your phone

Only what you deliberately send or share: a message to a player, or a draw file or list you export yourself. Everything else stays on the device. There is no sync, no backup to us, no iCloud component, and no leaderboard.

Children

HushDraw is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included. It is not directed at children under 13 as its primary audience, and it has no chat, no advertising, no user-generated content shared with strangers, and no web browsing.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. If a future version of the app ever collected anything, that change would be described here before it shipped.
